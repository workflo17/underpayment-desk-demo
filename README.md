# Underpayment Desk showcase

Underpayment Desk compares what insurers paid a medical practice with what the payer's contract says they owe, line by line, from the 835 remittance files the practice already receives. For every line paid short it names the clause, counts the appeal deadline and drafts the appeal. It computes New York prompt-pay interest on late claims and matches later payments to the appeals they answer. The biller approves and sends every appeal; the desk never submits anything.

This repository holds only the built showcase: https://workflo17.github.io/underpayment-desk-demo/

The showcase runs the real engine in your browser over 90 days of remittances from an invented practice and three invented payers. Its guided tour shows a fee schedule miss, a multiple-procedure cut on the wrong line, a line dropped as bundled that should pay, a correct bundle the desk refuses to appeal, a deadline two days away, prompt-pay interest, the appeal approval and a recovery. The practice, payers, patients and contracts are invented, and no figure is a typical result. Procedure codes appear as numbers only.
